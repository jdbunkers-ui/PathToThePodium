FANTASY WRESTLING FAQ

Questions before you draft?

Does Bracket Leaders conduct our fantasy draft?

No. Drafts are performed independently. Your commissioner uploads the completed fantasy team rosters for processing.

Does everyone in our league need an account?

No. Commissioners create an account to set up and administer their leagues. Individual league participants do not need an account to view results or standings.

How is fantasy scoring tracked?

Bracket Leaders processes NCAA wrestling match results and applies supported fantasy scoring rules, including advancement, placement, and bonus points, to calculate fantasy team scores and standings as results are processed.

Why could college team points differ from official NCAA standings?

Posted college team scores on Bracket Leaders do not include team penalties. Official NCAA team totals can therefore differ from the displayed calculated totals. This disclosure is about college team totals, not an additional fantasy scoring penalty.

Can we see previous tournament results?

Yes. Bracket Leaders publishes historical fantasy standings from 2025 onward, including FloWrestling and LMCC leagues. LMCC has run a fantasy league since 2018; the online archive begins in 2025.
